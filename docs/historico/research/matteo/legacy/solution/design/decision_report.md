# Decision approach: Sustainable Career v0.1

## Approach

Use current evidence to identify the most urgent constraint, address it proportionately, and pursue growth when the career remains sustainable. The priority is dynamic: recovery is appropriate when health is deteriorating, while capability, delivery or relationships may be more important under different feedback. Avoid both relentless short-term output maximization and indefinite defensive play.

For story events, compare the currently offered choices across immediate effects, delayed obligations, reversibility and the remaining career horizon. Prefer credible commitments and honest, constructive handling of work and relationships when supported by the actual context. Do not map named events to fixed answers. At quarterly phases, compare useful combinations of affordable Energy actions, reassessing after each, then choose one main action for the remaining need.

## Evidence and uncertainty

The approach uses the public handbook and rules, current visible state, current event text and choices, performance feedback and official recent logs. It does not inspect package contents or the simulator database. Unpublished promotion requirements, risk counters, event probabilities and score transformations remain unknown. A pattern seen in development is not treated as a guaranteed evaluation rule.

A 73,858-session random baseline documented elsewhere in the repository found frequent early health and trust failures. Those results motivate candidate responsibilities, not a claimed improvement. The full report and development scoring observations are not included in the Skills as agent decision rules.

## Principal design risks

- Excessive recovery could sacrifice capability and career progress. Resume growth after the active need is addressed.
- Visible statistics may omit liabilities. Consider warning text and recent commitments, not just the status panel.
- General workplace reasoning may predict an effect incorrectly. Observe after every action and update from actual feedback.
- Multiple Skills could add loading overhead or inconsistent priorities. Use one current risk assessment and a clear phase-specific route.
- A valid file structure does not prove the target model uses the instructions. Test discovery, loading, action validity and terminal handling in real runs.

## Generalization and evaluation

The candidate avoids event identifiers, memorized sequences, unpublished numerical thresholds and fixed schedules. It should remain applicable when event wording, order and parameters change, but that transfer has not been demonstrated.

Manual Codex-assisted play can reveal ambiguous guidance before a key is available. It changes the decision-maker and must be reported separately from official-model testing. The planned 100-run official-model target begins with a small operational and cost pilot; expansion depends on working setup and the remaining team credit. Track both survival and score, with failure causes and token use, rather than selecting a candidate on its single best run.
