Q3 · Why no scripts

No scripts. No reference files. On purpose.

Tried one

Best career at the sixth level, but 3 of 7 ended early and ~1.6× the tokens

Wrong problem

What fails is reading the options. A script can't read them better than the model

Never reopened

The agent reads the Skill early and doesn't open extra files
