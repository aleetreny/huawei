# Block 3 generation: weak-review streak and three candidates

Source: the 38 cloud Haiku digests (decision #114).

Reviews fall after months 5, 11, 17 and 23. A **good** review is a B or better, or a promotion. **At its high** means the highest value the panel showed at that Level in any digest: Output, Network and Skill stop rising within a level (lab analysis only, never Skill content).

The digests show 11 month-24 dismissals (C C D D ×8, C C D C ×2, C C C C ×1), not the status note's 14, which probably counts runs without a digest. One game with a B (845f5269-04) also ended at month 24, through a trust crisis.

## 1. The first promotion matters more than the grade

| First promotion | Games | Completed | Mean Q |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| review 1 | 14 | 12 | 57.3 |

- After the first review, no game got a B at Level 1: 0 of 47 such reviews (28 C, 19 D).
- All 12 games still at Level 1 after review 3 ended by month 24.
- The three games without a B that passed month 24 were all promoted at review 2, on a C.

## 2. Behaviour before the reviews is similar

Quarters up to month 23:

| | B by review 4 (22) | Dismissed (11) |
|---|---|---|
| Quarters with Prepare report / Defuse | 65 / 52% | 68 / 45% |
| Main action key project / report results | 61 / 15% | 61 / 19% |

| Months with such a remark | 21% | 30% |
| Story choices that cut Output or Network | 25% | 30% |

Every review 1 was a C, and its wording did not predict promotion: each of the three common texts appears in both groups (promoted/not 7/7, 4/9, 2/8). At review 2, Level 1 games not promoted had a "piled up"-or-worse remark in 11 of 13 half-years, against 5 of 11 promoted.

At review 1, Output was at its high in all 14 promoted games but in only 12 of the 24 others.

## 4. The loss window

After the last quarterly phase before a review, no Energy can undo a story choice that costs Output or Network. Reviews with such a drop were good in 14 of 60 cases, against 34 of 66 without one. Losing options: delays, pauses, side duties, other people's processes; gaining options: finishing, fixing or showing your own work. Caveat: Haiku takes the same option in an event under every policy, so the digests cannot isolate the option's effect.

## 5. The leader, f7-three-skills-v1

5 of 5 games completed (Q 50.4–71.0); both digests were promoted at review 1. e373478a-04 lost a year at Level 2 to two Cs: at review 2 Network had fallen to 5 (high 7); at review 3 a story choice after the quarterly phase had cut Output from 6 to 4. Of 6 reviews below Level 3, 3 followed such a loss. Notes marked Network or Output at risk while at its high 68 times in 156 story decisions, so the marks carry little information.

## 6. Chosen hypotheses

1. **f7-trend-marking-v1** (refine, 332 words): Skill, Network and Output are at risk only when below their recent high, not by size or grade; the main action covers what is still at risk, else a key project. Basis: §3, §5, Opus R3.
2. **f7-delivery-stories-v1** (refine, 372 words): while Output or Network is below its recent high, prefer finishing, fixing or showing your own work over delays and side duties, unless that hides a problem or Health is low. Basis: §4; parent marking kept.
3. **f7-promotion-drive-v1** (stall slot, playbook:P12, recombines F7 with f8-secure-growth, 389 words): while the latest review brought no promotion, delivered results and standing with decision makers come first, with a key project as main action; only Health, Dignity, Wealth and risk warnings override. Basis: §1; f8, which played this way before its first review, was promoted at review 1 in 3 of 4 games (14 of 38 overall). Contrarian to balance-first. Learning goal: does concentration bring the first promotion sooner, and at what cost in Network and risk?

## Not chosen

Energy or main-action changes (similar in both groups; the energy bots fix no review 1); R1 Defuse timing (pending cadence question); reacting to the review-1 text (not predictive); less Health protection (Health-driven story choices cut Output or Network at most 3 points more often than others in every Health band).
