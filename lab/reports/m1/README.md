## What stays and what changes

Preserved: honest but relationship-aware choices, preparation before review application, protection/restoration of Network and Output, capability growth, weak-review recovery, Health/Dignity/cash protection, avoidance of High-intensity work, redirecting investment toward the promotion blocker.

Changed:
- Replace "one zero gain proves a ceiling" with a provisional, level-specific hypothesis checked against individual actual outcomes and later feedback. Skill remains a possible blocker when Network and Output stay strong without promotion; it is not asserted to be the only possible blocker.
- Replace clearing historical risk when HR sounds calm with a separate incident record and explicit closure evidence. Escalating risk can override review growth.
- Replace a transient status line with a short self-contained checkpoint: RISK, REVIEW, OPEN, GAIN and PLAN, each carrying relevant public time/source. Reconcile the checkpoint with fresh observations and the selected choice.
- Recover public logs progressively when needed. Separate formal reviews from bonus messages, attempts from completion, advertised effects from actual pre/post values, and unknown from not reported.
- Require notes comfortably inside the public 1000-character limit with critical complete facts first. This is an actor instruction, not a machine-enforced guarantee. Do not depend on Continue to save notes.

The checkpoint deliberately retains only decision-relevant history. It cannot preserve every question ever asked. Delayed changes and simultaneous offsets prevent an immediate zero delta from proving an action is useless. No numeric promotion threshold or universal deterministic promotion rule is imported into M1.

## Evidence and limits

The separately fetched `matteo/confronto-v10` report (`lab/reports/matteo-observed-growth-v10-v12.md`) reports v10 completion 7/8, mean Q 59.56 and five L5 endings with Haiku. It also reports the intended capped-stat rule was not executed: "Capped: none" persisted and rotation never occurred at L4. These are parent results, not M1 results; the underlying eight games were not rerun or independently audited for this synthesis.

Today's public investigations are under `lab/reports/action-notes-study/`:
- Channel/contract/context and historical recovery support persistent logs, a 1000-character truncation limit, absence of Continue notes, and the need to distinguish entries from decisions. No universal expiry was identified.
- Cognition: curated checkpoints improve exact factual answers (119/126 versus 71/126 with history), but fail to win the predeclared answer-plus-citation comparison (42/126 versus 59/126). This supports checking provenance, not claiming a validated memory strategy.
- Agent-written follow-up: both budgets recover 0/6 historical facts in eight reused cases per budget; selection, source ambiguity, payoff aggregation and truncation are confounded. M1 responds to these failures but has not shown that the instructions fix them.
- The prior Luna champion-memory pilot ended at M24 without promotion. Neither that pilot nor curated-note recall establishes a promotion benefit.

M1 is longer than v10 and may increase repeated input/output cost. Persistent public notes are not provider KV cache. No cache lifetime or monetary saving is claimed. Its risk/review priority ordering and softer ceiling inference are substantive strategy changes, so a future result cannot be attributed to memory alone.
