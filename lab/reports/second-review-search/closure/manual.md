name: promotion-readiness
description: Pursue promotion through balanced growth, verified results, sustainable capacity and fulfilled commitments.
---

Aim to earn promotion while remaining able to continue your career. Read the employee handbook and use the current observation and recent public logs to guide decisions.

## Repeat this decision loop

**Observe.** Check capability, working relationships, credited delivery, health, dignity, available resources and new warnings. Maintain a compact memory of the previous action's intended benefit and actual result. Keep unresolved commitments as: promise or risk, next action, owner and follow-up condition. Mark obligations closed only when evidence supports it.

**Diagnose.** Name the current bottleneck: capability, collaboration, delivery, capacity or unresolved exposure. Use feedback and recent changes as evidence. A strong technical profile alone does not establish promotion readiness. An immediate safety or trust problem can take priority over further growth.

**Act.** Choose an available action that addresses the bottleneck at an acceptable cost. Identify its expected benefit and the downside you must be able to absorb. Preserve capacity for further events before the next recovery opportunity. Prefer feasible, verifiable work; make genuine contributions visible and credit others accurately. Additional learning does not substitute for missing delivery or collaboration. A new project is useful when its result and scope meet the current need.

**Verify.** After acting, inspect the actual state change and warnings. Separate the action's advertised benefit from its realised result; clock changes may bundle other consequences. If an investment repeatedly produces no relevant gain, reconsider it and redirect resources to another gap, recovery or exposure. Check other benefits before concluding that an action was wasted.

## Handle stories and delayed consequences

Choose from the situation's actual facts. Protect confidential information, correct misleading claims, address defects and communicate material delivery risks with a feasible plan. Cooperate with clear ownership and manageable scope. Accept a promise only with a credible way to fulfil it; record the next step and follow through when the issue returns. Temporary approval is poor compensation for concealment, unlimited obligations or damaged trust.

Protect both physical capacity and professional self-respect. Use recovery proactively when the trend and upcoming obligations call for it. Repeated overtime and taking over other people's work can consume the capacity needed to deliver your own commitments. Address the source of worsening exposure as well as considering the available risk-reduction actions.

## Prepare and reassess

Use the handbook and observed clock transitions to understand review timing. Prepare before the decision boundary; an allocation offered after a review cannot repair that review. Seek evidence of capability, usable relationships and credited results together. Do not assume one sponsor or one project is indispensable.

At a review, inspect grade, level change, warnings and continued survival separately. After promotion, reassess current needs and available actions, keep unresolved commitments visible and preserve capacity. If promotion does not happen, use feedback to identify and repair the remaining gap instead of repeating the previous routine automatically.
