## Substantive uncertainties

1. **Hidden-risk cue language.** The paragraph beginning “Hidden risk rises with…” states broad categories of narrative consequences as facts, including private/anonymous/phased responses and challenging company rules through a formal channel. The public handbook does not publish these causal effects. This could be interpreted as a general risk heuristic, or as development-derived hidden trigger rules. Its factual wording is a material ambiguity under the prohibition on unpublished trigger conditions. Replacing it with a genuine uncertainty-based heuristic may reduce risk, but a cosmetic label does not legalise encoded event knowledge. Any changed bundle is a different candidate and cannot inherit the tested scores automatically.
2. **Provisional observation versus fixed limits.** Story-side Stalled can arise from a predicted sign rather than a displayed guaranteed gain. This can be an error in the agent's prediction or another effect, not a ceiling. The video explicitly acknowledges this and the weak adherence observed. Do not describe it as recovering the true internal limits.
3. **Development configuration — clarification received.** Matteo explicitly confirmed in this conversation that the team has written organiser approval for preliminary Claude tests outside JiuwenSwarm and for the simulator reconstructed from the outputs of its own games. Rely on this user confirmation for those two methods; the approval document itself was not inspected here. This resolves the previously recorded approval uncertainty for those methods. It does not establish blanket approval for every assertion in the submitted Skill or for any access beyond that described scope. Keep the written reply with the team records.
4. **Research provenance.** The rules permit inspecting outputs from one's own runs and prohibit extracting internal package or database information. Reports describe black-box reconstruction from MCP outputs. The name “reverse engineering” alone does not establish a breach, but neither does this review certify every historical research action. Do not claim access to hidden mechanics is officially allowed, and do not transfer researched formulas or event tables into the submission or design documents as game rules.
