## Decisions and rationale

- **One executable Skill:** a turn loop, ordered priorities, named quarterly actions and story rules. Short action notes and small retained state aim to reduce cognitive and token load.
- **Contextual risk signals:** actual accumulated exposure or unresolved problems trigger cleanup; institutional words alone do not. This may prevent excessive defence, but may also miss subtle warnings. Both errors need trace audits.
- **Recovery before collapse:** worsening trends, symptoms and foreseeable costs matter. Our growth pilot reached health 1, so recovery should not wait for an arbitrary numerical threshold. Perfect wellbeing is not required for growth.
- **Review and risk coexist:** necessary defensive Energy can coexist with a corrective main action; immediate survival comes first. Weak reviews do not force an indefinite defensive main action.
- **Observe realized benefits:** do not repeat a nominal improvement without checking actual effects. An unchanged statistic is not proof of a hidden cap; narrative or other benefits can still justify the action.
- **Growth through outcomes:** use key projects for ownership, evidence for recognition, and clear role/pay terms where offered. Prefer lower-cost delivery evidence when public action effects support that comparison.
