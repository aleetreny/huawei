# Matteo T4: responsibility converted into career value

## Proposal

Branch `matteo-t4-growth` contains a single independently written candidate at `lab/policies-src/matteo-value-conversion/value-conversion-v1`. A compact Skill treats sustainable responsibility, delivery evidence and explicit recognition or compensation as one chain. It seeks growth through feasible commitments and offered negotiation opportunities, while responding to urgent wellbeing, risk and review concerns.

This is a new hypothesis, not a proven best policy. Packaging follows the requested f3-lean-v1 template; its instructions were replaced, not refined. No central candidate Skill text or teacher digest was read to draft this proposal. The author has seen broad central research conclusions and template metadata, and has interpreted previous Skills in manual games: this is not a blind outside-model experiment. It may overlap with central growth candidates; the organiser should check redundancy before spending model quota.

## Evidence and limits

Our historical manual pilots scored 69.4 and 73.4, both ending at L5 with identical promotion months. These motivate investigating how growth becomes recognised responsibility or income, but neither evaluated this candidate and neither estimates its effect. No game was launched for T4. No claimed numerical promotion requirement or event-specific response enters the bundle.

Use the cloud's frozen evaluation protocol and actor. Inspect promotion times, level, wealth, completion, total score, risk and tokens, alongside adherence to the scope/evidence/negotiation mechanism. A more verbose model can make a weak manual look effective; do not pool prior Codex scores with lab actor samples. Failure criteria and expected behaviour are recorded in policy.json before evaluation.
