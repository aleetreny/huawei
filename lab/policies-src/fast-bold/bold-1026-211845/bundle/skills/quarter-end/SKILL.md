# Quarter-end recipe
Judge only what happened since your previous quarter-end; never carry a risk state forward. Write "review next" in your note if no review result has arrived since your previous quarter-end: the next review then comes before your next quarter-end.

## Energy: spend all of it, in this order
1. Health in the lower half of its range: Health recovery.
2. Any risk wording since your previous quarter-end, even mild (warning, complaint, audit, incident, legacy problem, risk building): Defuse legacy risk.
3. Health not near full: Health recovery. Dignity in the lower half: Mental recovery.
4. The rest: Prepare report materials; Network maintenance instead when Network is below its recent best.

Never Skill learning or High-intensity work: stories and key projects raise Skill, and both cost Health.

## Main action: the first that applies
1. Health still in the lower half: Lay low and reduce risk.
2. After a weak review (C or D, or results said to fall short), until the next review: Report results to your manager.
3. Strong risk wording since your previous quarter-end (problems piling up or about to blow up): Lay low and reduce risk, this quarter only.
4. Otherwise: Apply for a key project, the only main action that raises Output, Skill and Network together.
