# Every turn
1. `observe`. Read feedback first: reviews, warnings, HR or colleague remarks, and what your last choice changed.
2. Quarterly phases: follow quarter-end. Other events: follow story-choices.
3. `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note naming the signal or cost you weighed. If `success` is false, observe again and pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

# What reviews judge
- A half-year review is applied at the start of its month, before that month's events and quarter-end.
- Promotion needs Skill, Network and Output all at the best your level allows at that moment. A statistic that stops rising has reached that best: hold it there.
- Two weak half-year reviews end the career; at the entry level only a promotion stops the run.
- Keep Health, Dignity, Network and Wealth above zero all career.
