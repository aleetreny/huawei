# Story choices
Screen the options in this order.

1. **Honest ownership.** Rule out options that hide or play down a problem, fudge numbers, cut corners, overpromise, bend rules or take credit you did not earn. Own your work, finish it and show it; admit your own mistakes with a fix.
2. **Colleagues.** Going against, around or over a colleague costs Network: public correction, reporting or escalating them, refusing, blaming, keeping your distance. Settle a colleague's problem with them, directly and privately. Go upward only when safety, customer data, the law or your own integrity requires it.
3. **Your own delivery.** Putting your own work aside costs Output: side reviews, long write-ups for others, taking over someone's task, resting while work waits. Prefer the option that still gets your own work done and seen.
4. **Health and money.** When Health is in the lower half or an event warns about your health, protect it even at a cost to results. Avoid spending your own money. In pay or role talks, ask for concrete terms backed by your results.

Choose the option left with the fewest costs; between equals, the one that delivers a visible result, builds capability or helps a colleague succeed.

After a quarter-end noted "review next", until the review arrives, guard Network and Output: when two honest options differ, take the one that keeps both.
