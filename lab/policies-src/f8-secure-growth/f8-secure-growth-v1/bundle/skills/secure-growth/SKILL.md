## Turn loop
1. `observe`; read `events` and your last action's result first.
2. Set the mode, then `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note: mode, signal seen, response.
3. If `success` is false, pick an affordable option. Stop at `ending_score`.

## Modes
Check in this order; several can hold at once.
1. **Recover**: Health or Dignity low or named in a warning. Health recovery or Mental recovery comes first; no High-intensity work.
2. **Secure**: a recent risk signal (warning, HR, compliance, complaint, audit or legacy-problem wording, a statistic warning, or a rising risk entry in `status_updates`). Defuse legacy risk first in the next Energy phase, and in every Energy phase while signals repeat. In story events prefer disclosing, fixing or closing a problem over postponing, hiding or shifting it. Leave Secure after a whole quarter without a new risk signal.
3. **Repair**: after a C or D, or feedback that results fall short, until the next review. Aim the main action at the factor named (results: Report results to your manager; capability: Build a mentorship; relationships: Repair a key relationship) and spend spare Energy on Prepare report materials. Repair decides the main action even while Secure holds.
4. **Grow**: none of the above. Play for promotion and pay.
   - Main action: Apply for a key project; Showcase results once a project has delivered.
   - Energy: High-intensity work while Health is comfortable, plus Prepare report materials; Skill learning when Skill trails Output.
   - Story events: prefer options that raise Output, Skill, Wealth or standing with decision makers at a bounded cost over options that only keep things comfortable. Still refuse options that hide problems, add risk wording or push Health or Dignity low.

Keep Wealth above zero.
