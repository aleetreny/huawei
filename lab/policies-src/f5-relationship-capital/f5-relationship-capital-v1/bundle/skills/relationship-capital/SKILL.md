# Relationship capital

Strong relationships and standing protect a career: they soften conflicts, support reviews and promotion, and the ending considers network and dignity. Build them first, without letting the other dimensions fail.

## Turn loop
`observe` → decide → `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a note naming the relationship or standing effect. If `success` is false, observe and pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Story events
Rank the offered options by these questions, in order:
1. Does it keep trust (honest, transparent, keeps promises, shares credit, no blame-shifting)?
2. Does it coordinate (involves the right people, aligns with the manager, escalates through proper channels, asks for help early)?
3. Does it protect Dignity (respectful boundaries; does not silently accept humiliation or misconduct)?
4. Does it avoid a Health cost or an obligation you cannot meet?
Choose the best combined answer. When every option damages a relationship, choose the one easiest to repair and plan Repair a key relationship as the next main action.

## Quarterly Energy
1. Health recovery comes first whenever Health is in the lower part of its 0–10 range or falling.
2. Otherwise start with Network maintenance, which raises Network and Dignity.
3. Mental recovery when Dignity is the lowest principal statistic or falling.
4. Spare points: Prepare report materials (Output and Dignity), or Skill learning while Health is comfortable and Skill is the lagging statistic.
5. Include Defuse legacy risk after a risk warning.
Use the `energy_cost` and `remaining_energy` shown; spend all Energy while a useful affordable action remains.

## Quarterly main action
- After conflict, trust or relationship feedback: Repair a key relationship.
- Otherwise choose between Build a mentorship and Report results to your manager, whichever covers the weaker of Skill and Output.
- Lay low and reduce risk only when Health needs it.

## Keep the rest adequate
Relationships do not replace results. After a C or D review, make Report results to your manager the main action until the next review. Keep Wealth above zero and Skill growing, because falling behind on either can end the run. Apply the same priorities until the run ends.
