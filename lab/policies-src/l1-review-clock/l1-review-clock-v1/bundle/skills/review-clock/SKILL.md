## Turn loop
1. `observe`. Read `events` and the result of your last action before the choices.
2. Decide with the rules below, then `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note: reserve, risk tone, window plan.
3. If `success` is false, pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Health reserve
Story events can cost Health suddenly and recovery only comes at quarter end. Keep a reserve: in each Energy phase, spend on Health recovery until Health is near the top of its range, and on Mental recovery if Dignity has fallen to the middle. Recover before Health is low, not after. In story events, an option that costs Health is expensive while Health is not high.

## Risk tone
The HR partner's monthly remark and any warning are your risk gauge. A relaxed tone means clear. A worried tone, piled-up risks, or any warning means act: Defuse legacy risk with spare Energy, and in story events choose options that disclose, fix or close a problem. After a blow-up, Defuse in every Energy phase and pick Lay low and reduce risk as the main action until the tone is relaxed again.

## Window plan
Promotion considers Skill, Network and Output, and each review grades sustained results. Grow all three every quarter:
- Main action: Apply for a key project. Use Lay low and reduce risk only when Health is low. If one of the three clearly trails, use the matching action instead: Build a mentorship for Skill or Network, Report results to your manager for Output.
- Spare Energy after the reserve: Prepare report materials. Use Skill learning only while Health is full. Never High-intensity work: it adds hidden risk and drains Health.
- After a C or D grade, read what the feedback names (results, capability or relationships) and aim the next two main actions at it.

## Story events
Prefer options that deliver real work with honest, scoped commitments and keep the team on side. Avoid options that hide, blame, deceive, take unearned credit or promise unlimited extra hours, even when they look rewarding. Keep Wealth and Network above zero.
