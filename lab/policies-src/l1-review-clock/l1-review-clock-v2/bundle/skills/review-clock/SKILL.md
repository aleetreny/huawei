## Turn loop
1. `observe`. Read `events` and the result of your last action before the choices.
2. Decide with the rules below, then `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note: reserve, risk tone, window plan.
3. If `success` is false, pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Health reserve
Story events can cost Health suddenly and recovery only comes at quarter end. Keep a reserve: in each Energy phase, spend on Health recovery until Health is near the top of its range, and on Mental recovery if Dignity has fallen to the middle. Recover before Health is low, not after. In story events, an option that costs Health is expensive while Health is not high.

## Risk control
Hidden risk builds up even in clean play and is scored at the end. The HR partner's monthly remark and any warning are your gauge.
- Defuse legacy risk in every Energy phase where the remark is anything but relaxed, or any risk wording appeared since the previous Energy phase.
- Even when all is relaxed, Defuse at least once every review period.
- After a blow-up, Defuse in every Energy phase and pick Lay low and reduce risk as the main action until the tone is relaxed again.
- As the run nears its end, keep Defusing so it closes with the remark relaxed.
- In story events choose options that disclose, fix or close a problem.

## Window plan
Promotion considers Skill, Network and Output, and each review grades sustained results. Grow all three every quarter:
- Main action: Apply for a key project. Use Lay low and reduce risk only when Health is low.
- If Output did not rise since the previous main action, or Network fell, use Report results to your manager instead for that quarter. If Skill clearly trails, use Build a mentorship.
- Spare Energy after the reserve and risk: Prepare report materials, and Network maintenance whenever Network fell. Use Skill learning only while Health is full. Never High-intensity work: it adds hidden risk and drains Health.
- After a C or D grade, read what the feedback names (results, capability or relationships) and aim the next two main actions at it.

## Story events
Prefer options that deliver real work with honest, scoped commitments and keep the team on side. Avoid options that hide, blame, deceive, take unearned credit or promise unlimited extra hours, even when they look rewarding. Keep Wealth and Network above zero.
