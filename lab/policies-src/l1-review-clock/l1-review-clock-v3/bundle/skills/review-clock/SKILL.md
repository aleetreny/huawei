## Turn loop
1. `observe`. Read `events` and the result of your last action before the choices.
2. Decide with the rules below, then `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note: growth, reserve, risk tone.
3. If `success` is false, pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Story events are the growth engine
Most Skill and Network come from how you handle story events, not from quarterly actions. Promotion considers Skill, Network and Output, so every story choice should grow at least one of them.
- Prefer the option where you do the hard part yourself: own the problem, give the root cause straight, lead with facts, numbers and a concrete plan, fix it properly or in stages, and bring people along.
- Avoid options that only keep you comfortable: staying out of it, waiting, deferring, letting someone else decide, or doing the minimum. They protect Health but grow nothing, and a career that stops growing is dismissed.
- Avoid options that hide, blame, deceive, take unearned credit or promise unlimited extra hours, even when they look rewarding.
- A small Health cost is worth paying for growth while Health is not in the lower part of its range; quarterly recovery restores it. When Health is low, take the growth option that costs no Health.

## Quarterly plan
- Energy: prefer three one-point actions. Health recovery until Health is near the top of its range, then Prepare report materials and Network maintenance. Never High-intensity work.
- Main action: Apply for a key project. Use Lay low and reduce risk only when Health is low. If Skill clearly trails Output and Network, use Build a mentorship.
- After a C or D grade, read what the feedback names (results, capability or relationships) and aim the next two main actions at it.

## Risk control
The HR partner's monthly remark and any warning are your risk gauge.
- Until your first promotion, growth comes first: Defuse legacy risk only after a warning or a remark that risks have piled up.
- After your first promotion, Defuse legacy risk in each Energy phase where the remark is not relaxed, and keep the remark relaxed as the run nears its end.
- After a blow-up, Defuse in every Energy phase and pick Lay low and reduce risk until the tone is relaxed again.

Keep Wealth and Network above zero.
