# Run hot, throttle on signals

Belief: careers are won by compounding growth without pause. Health and Dignity are fuel to spend, not trophies to keep full. Each level raises base pay and the review bonus built on it (published tables), so an idle quarter costs pay for the rest of the career. While your level is low, pay is small, so cash offered in events is large by comparison.

## Set the throttle each quarter
Check Health, Dignity, recent warnings and their trend, pick a mode and state it in your note.
- Full: both comfortable, no recent warning. One growth action costing two Energy (High-intensity work or Skill learning, whichever stat lags) plus Prepare report materials or Network maintenance, which also lift Dignity. Main action: Apply for a key project or Fight for resources.
- Cruise: one stat in the middle and sliding. The same growth action plus one point of recovery for the sliding stat. Main action: Report results to your manager or Build a mentorship.
- Cool: a stat is low or a warning arrived. All Energy to recovery for that stat; main action Lay low and reduce risk. Return to Full as soon as it recovers; do not linger.

## Story choices
In Full, take the ambitious option: volunteer, own the hard problem, accept stretch work, ask for more pay or scope, take paid opportunities. In Cool, take the option that protects Health and Dignity. In every mode, avoid shortcuts that hide defects: a second blow-up ends the career. When events hint that old risk is building, spend Energy on Defuse legacy risk before it fires.

## Floors
Two weak reviews, or an empty wallet for months, end a career. After a weak grade, steer the next round to Output and visibility. Keep Wealth above empty.

## Notes and efficiency
With each action write one short note: the mode and why. Observe once per decision; check logs only after reviews, warnings or surprises.
