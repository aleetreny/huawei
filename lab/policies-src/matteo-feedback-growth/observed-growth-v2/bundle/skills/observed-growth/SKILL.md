## Why growth comes first
The handbook ties promotion to Skill, Network, Output and level, and pay and review bonuses rise with level. An early promotion compounds: later work builds stats faster. A career stuck at its starting level loses on level, wealth and reviews together. Survival means good reviews, and good reviews come from growing stats.

## Turn loop
Read the handbook once at the start and note when quarter-end allocations, formal reviews and promotion windows happen. Each turn: observe, read feedback and the event, choose an offered affordable option, act with a short note, observe again. If an action is rejected, observe before retrying. Stop at the explicit career ending.

## Story events: the main growth engine
Most stat gains come from story choices. Prefer the option that takes ownership, delivers a concrete result, builds capability, helps a colleague visibly or makes your contribution known. Avoid options that withdraw, decline responsibility, stay silent or merely wait, unless taking part would cause a serious Health drop or an integrity, safety or confidentiality breach. Stay honest: disclose real defects and fix them completely.

## Quarter-end allocation
At every quarter-end, write in your note the current Skill, Network and Output, their values at the previous quarter-end, and whether a formal review follows this allocation.

**Build quarter** (no review follows): grow capability.
- Energy: Skill learning, then Network maintenance or Prepare report materials.
- Main action: Apply for rotation or Build a mentorship; Apply for a key project when Output lags.

**Review quarter** (a review and promotion window follow): make results visible.
- Energy: Prepare report materials; High-intensity work only when Health is strong.
- Main action: Report results to your manager or Showcase results; Apply for a key project when it adds Output.

**Energy discipline**
- Health recovery only when Health limits the planned spending or shows real strain. Do not top up a healthy reserve.
- Defuse legacy risk only for a concrete exposure you own: a defect, an unresolved incident or a direct warning about your conduct. A remark that some risk exists but is not serious does not qualify, and does not justify it in a review quarter.
- Lay low only for an immediate threat.

**Realized-benefit check**
If Skill, Network and Output did not rise since the previous quarter-end, change the mix this quarter: swap the repeated main action for a different growth action and move Energy from recovery or cleanup to Skill learning or report materials.

## After a weak review
A second weak review ends the career, so the next review decides survival. Until it, every quarter targets the weakness the review named plus visible Output, in both quarter types. No Defuse without a concrete exposure and no Lay low. Keep enough Health to act, nothing more.

## Wealth
Promotion is the main source of wealth. In offered pay or role talks, cite actual results and market evidence and ask for concrete terms. Accept bounded extra responsibility that comes with recognition.
