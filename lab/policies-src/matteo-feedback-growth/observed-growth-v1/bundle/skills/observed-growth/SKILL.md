## Turn loop
Observe; read feedback, the event and the last outcome. Retain only the latest review concern, unresolved warnings, commitments and observed investment benefits. Select an offered, affordable choice. Act with a short note: priority, evidence, choice. Observe after acting. If rejected or uncertain whether an action applied, observe before retrying. Stop at the explicit career ending.

## Priority order
1. Protect wellbeing when symptoms, a worsening trend or known upcoming costs threaten the available reserve. Use Health recovery or Mental recovery for the threatened dimension. Avoid further strain until the threat is addressed; full wellbeing is not a prerequisite for growth.
2. Address concrete unresolved risk. A warning about your accumulated exposure, an actual defect or a pending obligation counts; merely mentioning HR, compliance or an audit does not. Prefer truthful disclosure, a complete remedy and follow-through over concealment or postponement. Use Defuse legacy risk at the next affordable Energy opportunity. Reassess after new feedback; a reassuring remark alone does not close a known unresolved problem.
3. After a C or D, target the weakness named by the review until later feedback resolves it. Keep corrective main actions active while spending Energy on necessary recovery or risk control. An immediate survival threat overrides this plan.
4. Otherwise pursue promotion readiness and compensation through useful ownership, credible results and collaboration.

## Quarterly decisions
Read the current costs and effects; reassess after every expenditure.
- Results gap: Prepare report materials; choose Apply for a key project for ownership or Report results to your manager for visibility of completed work.
- Capability gap: Skill learning if its health cost is sustainable; Apply for rotation for broader experience.
- Relationship gap: Network maintenance; Build a mentorship for collaboration, or Repair a key relationship for an actual rupture.
- With no named gap, choose Apply for a key project and spend spare Energy on useful delivery evidence. Prefer Prepare report materials over High-intensity work when it supplies the needed gain with less strain and risk.
- If an investment leaves its intended statistic unchanged, do not automatically repeat it. Check feedback and other benefits; switch to another relevant gap when no benefit is evident. Do not infer a hidden ceiling. Choose Lay low and reduce risk only for an unresolved threat, not as the growth default.

## Story decisions
Accept bounded learning and wider responsibility when feasible. Prefer explicit scope, resources, handovers and review terms. Complete commitments, give accurate shared credit and make contributions visible. In offered pay or role negotiations, use actual results and market evidence; seek concrete terms rather than vague promises. Preserve confidentiality and address serious defects before they become unresolved liabilities.
