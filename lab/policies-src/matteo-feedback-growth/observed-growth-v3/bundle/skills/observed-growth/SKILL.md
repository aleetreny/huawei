## Why growth comes first
The handbook ties promotion to Skill, Network, Output and level, and pay and review bonuses rise with level. Promotion also lets stats keep growing: a stat can stall at your current level and resume after the next promotion. Survival means good reviews, and good reviews come from growing stats. Two things end careers anyway: hidden risk that blows up, and exhausted Health.

## Turn loop
Read the handbook once at the start and note when quarter-end allocations, formal reviews and promotion windows happen. Each turn: observe, read feedback and the event, choose an offered affordable option, act with a short note, observe again. If an action is rejected, observe before retrying. Stop at the explicit career ending.

## Story events
Most stat gains come from story choices. Prefer the option that takes ownership, delivers a concrete result, builds capability, helps a colleague visibly or makes your contribution known. Never choose options that cut corners, overpromise, hide a problem, take undue credit or bend rules: they feed hidden risk. Decline only when taking part would cause a serious Health drop or an integrity, safety or confidentiality breach. Disclose real defects and fix them completely.

## Quarter-end allocation
Write in your note: current Skill, Network, Output and Health; their values at the previous quarter-end; any risk signal since then; whether a formal review follows this allocation. Then allocate in this order.

1. **Risk cleanup.** If HR, your manager, a doctor or an event mentioned risk, warnings, piled-up issues or an incident since the previous quarter-end, spend Defuse legacy risk first, even when the remark says it is not serious yet. Its effect is invisible but real.
2. **Health reserve.** Spend Health recovery when Health is in the lower half of its range, or when the planned spending would put it there. Do not recover a near-full reserve.
3. **Growth** with the remaining Energy and the main action:
   - Build quarter (no review follows): Skill learning, Network maintenance; main action Apply for rotation, Build a mentorship or Apply for a key project.
   - Review quarter (a review and promotion window follow): Prepare report materials; main action Report results to your manager, Showcase results or Apply for a key project.
   - If Skill did not rise since the previous quarter-end, it has stalled at this level: skip Skill learning until the next promotion and put that Energy into Prepare report materials or Network maintenance.
   - If Skill, Network and Output all stayed flat, swap the repeated main action for a different growth action.

## After a weak review
A second weak review ends the career, so the next review decides survival. Until it, every quarter targets the weakness the review named plus visible Output. Keep risk cleanup and the Health reserve; drop everything else that does not grow a promotion input. Do not Lay low.

## Wealth
Promotion is the main source of wealth. In offered pay or role talks, cite actual results and market evidence and ask for concrete terms. Accept bounded extra responsibility that comes with recognition.
