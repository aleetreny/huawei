## Why growth comes first
The handbook ties promotion to Skill, Network, Output and level, and pay and review bonuses rise with level and grade. Promotion also lets stats keep growing: a stat can stall at your current level and resume after the next promotion. Good reviews come from growing stats. Two things end careers anyway: hidden risk that blows up, and exhausted Health.

## Turn loop
Read the handbook once at the start and note when quarter-end allocations, formal reviews and promotion windows happen. Each turn: observe, read feedback and the event, choose an offered affordable option, act with a short note, observe again. If an action is rejected, observe before retrying. Stop at the explicit career ending.

## Story events
Most stat gains come from story choices. Prefer the option that takes ownership, delivers a concrete result, builds capability, helps a colleague visibly or makes your contribution known. When an option secures concrete pay, a bonus, a raise or a role with recognition on fair terms, prefer it. Never choose options that cut corners, overpromise, hide a problem, take undue credit or bend rules: they feed hidden risk. Decline only when taking part would cause a serious Health drop or an integrity, safety or confidentiality breach. Disclose real defects and fix them completely.

## Quarter-end allocation
Write a short note: Skill, Network, Output and Health now and at the previous quarter-end; risk signals since then; whether a formal review follows. Then allocate in this order.

1. **Risk cleanup.** Spend Defuse legacy risk when there was an incident, a defect, or a remark that risk or issues have piled up since the previous quarter-end. A mild remark that some risk exists but is not serious also counts, unless you already spent Defuse at the previous quarter-end and nothing else happened since. Its effect is invisible but real.
2. **Health reserve.** Spend Health recovery when Health is in the lower half of its range, or when the planned spending would put it there. Do not recover a near-full reserve.
3. **Push the stalled input.** Of Skill, Network and Output, find the one that rose least since the previous quarter-end; it is the likely bottleneck for the next promotion. Spend the remaining Energy and the main action on it:
   - Skill: Skill learning; Apply for rotation or Build a mentorship. If Skill did not rise at all, it has stalled at this level: switch to Output or Network until the next promotion.
   - Network: Network maintenance; Build a mentorship or Repair a key relationship.
   - Output: Prepare report materials, High-intensity work only when Health is near full; Fight for resources, Showcase results or Apply for a key project.
   If no review follows, Skill and Network may take priority. If a review follows, make results visible: include Prepare report materials and a main action that adds Output or reports results. If all three stayed flat, swap the repeated main action for a different one.

## Reviews and pay
Aim for strong grades, not just a pass: bonuses scale with grade. A second weak review ends the career, so after a weak review every quarter targets the named weakness plus visible Output until the next review. Keep risk cleanup and the Health reserve; do not Lay low. In offered pay or role talks, cite actual results and market evidence and ask for concrete terms.
