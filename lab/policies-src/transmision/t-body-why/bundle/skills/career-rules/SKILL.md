## Turn loop
1. `observe`; read `events` first (HR remark, review talk, warnings).
2. Find the rule that applies below, then `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note.
3. If `success` is false, pick another option. Stop when the game ends.

## Rules
1. **Protect the review.** Reviews come every six months. The review is settled as the review month begins, before that month's stories, so the story choices of the month just before a review month are the last ones that count. In that month never pick an option that may lower Skill, Network or Output, even if another option looks more principled or adds Dignity: Dignity and Health can be rebuilt later, a lost point can cost the promotion. Options that confront, correct or refuse someone in public usually cost Network; options that take time from your own delivery (extra unplanned work, investigations, rework, helping outside your role) usually cost Output.
   Why: a promotion needs Skill, Network and Output all at the level's bar on the day of the review; in the first levels the bar sits at the top of what the level lets you hold, so there is no spare point.
2. **Build Skill at senior levels.** From L4 on, Skill is the slow stat that holds promotion back; Network and Output keep up on their own. Every quarter at L4 or above, spend 2 Energy on Skill learning and take Apply for rotation as the main action, unless the risk rule or low Health says otherwise. In stories, prefer the option that teaches or builds capability.
   Why: the handbook says higher levels set a higher bar; the bar that rises fastest is Skill, and quarterly Skill actions are the only steady source besides stories.
3. **Answer the HR remark.** Each month an HR partner comments on your risk. Only the smiling, all-right remark means clear. Any remark that mentions risk, worry or anger means hidden risk is piling up; it blows up when it gets high, and a second blow-up ends the run. While the remark is not the clear one: in the quarter take Lay low and reduce risk as the main action and Defuse legacy risk with Energy, until the clear remark comes back.
   Why: the remark is the only visible reading of hidden risk; acting early is cheap, a blow-up costs Dignity, Wealth and half a year of Output.
4. **No detonators under risk.** While the HR remark is not the clear one, never pick an option that hides, denies, deceives, gossips, cuts corners, delays a disclosure, or puts you under formal scrutiny (formal complaints, investigations, open letters, escalating over your manager's head). These set off the buried risk at once. Prefer the option that discloses, fixes or closes the problem quietly.
   Why: with risk already buried, an option that could expose it or invite scrutiny makes it go off on the spot; with a clear remark the same option is usually safe.
5. **Do not go broke early.** Pay is small in the first levels and Wealth starts near zero. While Wealth is only a few months of pay, refuse options that cost money (paying, buying, treating, lending, fees, gifts). Months with Wealth below zero end the run.
   Why: early pay barely covers small losses, and an empty wallet held for a few months is a run-ending failure.
6. **Close the career well.** The ending evaluation counts level, wealth, health and dignity, skill, risk control and network. In the last three months of the 48: bring the HR remark back to clear (Lay low and reduce risk, Defuse legacy risk), restore Health and Dignity with Energy, and in stories pick the option that keeps you healthy, respected and out of trouble; take on no new risk.
   Why: in the last months there is no later review to play for; only the ending evaluation is left.
