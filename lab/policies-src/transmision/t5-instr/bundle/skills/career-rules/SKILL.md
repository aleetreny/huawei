## Turn loop
1. `observe`; read `events` first (HR remark, review talk, warnings).
2. Find the rule that applies below, then `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note.
3. If `success` is false, pick another option. Stop when the game ends.

## Rules
Rules are listed in priority order: when two apply, the earlier one wins.

1. **Close the career well.** The career ends after month 48 (the published length); the ending evaluation counts level, wealth, health and dignity, skill, risk control and network. In the quarterly phase of that final month: main action Lay low and reduce risk; Energy first on Defuse legacy risk if the HR remark is not clear, then Health recovery and Mental recovery until Health and Dignity are full, then Network maintenance. Never High-intensity work or Skill learning there: their gains come too late and their Health cost counts. In the stories of the last months, pick the option that keeps you healthy, respected and out of trouble.
2. **Keep Health and Dignity safe.** Health at zero ends the run at once, and Dignity left near zero ends it too. When Health is low (4 or less out of 10): in stories never pick an option that costs Health (overtime, pushing through illness, skipping rest), and spend Energy on Health recovery before anything else. When Dignity is low (3 or less), Mental recovery comes first. Skill learning and High-intensity work cost Health: skip them while Health is low.
3. **Answer the HR remark.** Each month an HR partner comments on your risk. Only the smiling, all-right remark means clear. Any remark that mentions risk, worry or anger means hidden risk is piling up; it blows up when it gets high, and a second blow-up ends the run. While the remark is not the clear one, in every quarterly phase: the first Energy action is Defuse legacy risk (unless Health is nearly gone) and the main action is Lay low and reduce risk. This comes before growth, before Skill and before any review plan.
4. **Protect the review.** Reviews come every six months. Treat the month before each review month as the deadline: the review talk can come as the review month opens, before its stories, so plan as if that month's stories will not count. In the month before a review month never pick an option that may lower Skill, Network or Output, even if another option looks more principled or adds Dignity. Before choosing, mark each option: does it go against a person (blaming, correcting, refusing, reporting someone)? Then it costs Network. Does it take time from your own delivery (extra or unplanned work, investigations, rework, the slow thorough path, stopping to fix someone else's problem)? Then it costs Output. Pick an option with neither mark; if every option has one, pick the one whose loss is on a stat that is not holding your promotion back.
5. **No detonators under risk.** While the HR remark is not the clear one, strike out every option that hides or denies something, lies or invents, gossips or leaks, cuts a corner, or starts a formal process (formal complaints, investigations, open letters, going over your manager's head). The more worried the remark, the more you prefer the quietest option left that is not dishonest. Use your own judgement among the options that remain.
6. **Do not go broke early.** Pay is small in the first levels and Wealth starts near zero. While Wealth is only a few months of pay, refuse options that cost money (paying, buying, treating, lending, fees, gifts). Months with Wealth below zero end the run.
7. **Build Skill at senior levels.** From L4 on, the review bar rises fastest for Skill; watch whether reviews stall while Network and Output keep growing. In every quarterly phase at L4 or above (except the career's final month), spend 2 Energy on Skill learning and take Apply for rotation as the main action, unless a rule above says otherwise. In stories, prefer the option that teaches or builds capability.

## Reading what an option costs
Option effects are hidden; read them from the wording. Invented examples (not from the game):
- "Correct the director in front of the client" lowers Network. "Raise it with the director privately afterwards" keeps it.
- "Spend the week rebuilding a colleague's slides" lowers Output: your own delivery slips. "Point them to the template and finish your release" keeps it.
- "Agree to the impossible date to keep the peace" or "say nothing and hope" add hidden risk. "State calmly what can be done by when" is safe.
- "Quietly fix it and tell nobody" hides a problem: hidden risk. "Fix it and note it in the ticket" is safe.
