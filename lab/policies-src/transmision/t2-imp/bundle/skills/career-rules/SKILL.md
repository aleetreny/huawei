## Turn loop
1. `observe`; read `events` first (HR remark, review talk, warnings).
2. Find the rule that applies below, then `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note.
3. If `success` is false, pick another option. Stop when the game ends.

## Rules
1. **Protect the review.** Reviews come every six months and are settled as the review month begins, before that month's stories, so the story choices of the month just before a review month are the last ones that count. In that month never pick an option that may lower Skill, Network or Output, even if another option looks more principled or adds Dignity. Network is lost by blaming, accusing, refusing help or turning against someone in public; Output is lost by dropping or pausing your own delivery for extra unplanned work, investigations or rework. Keeping your view calmly and delivering your own work protects both; caving in is not safer.
2. **Build Skill at senior levels.** From L4 on, Skill is the slow stat that holds promotion back; Network and Output keep up on their own. Every quarter at L4 or above, spend 2 Energy on Skill learning and take Apply for rotation as the main action, unless the risk rule or low Health says otherwise. In stories, prefer the option that teaches or builds capability.
3. **Answer the HR remark.** Each month an HR partner comments on your risk. Only the smiling, all-right remark means clear. Any remark that mentions risk, worry or anger means hidden risk is piling up; it blows up when it gets high, and a second blow-up ends the run. While the remark is not the clear one, in every quarterly phase: the first Energy action is Defuse legacy risk (unless Health is nearly gone) and the main action is Lay low and reduce risk. This comes before growth, before Skill and before any review plan.
4. **No detonators under risk.** While the HR remark is not the clear one, never pick an option that hides or denies something, lies or invents, gossips or mocks, cuts a corner, stays silent to avoid trouble, caves in or agrees just to please, or blames someone in public; and do not start formal processes (formal complaints, investigations, open letters, going over your manager's head). Pick the option that is honest, calm and owns the problem: stating your view respectfully is safe, backing down to please is not.
5. **Do not go broke early.** Pay is small in the first levels and Wealth starts near zero. While Wealth is only a few months of pay, refuse options that cost money (paying, buying, treating, lending, fees, gifts). Months with Wealth below zero end the run.
6. **Close the career well.** The ending evaluation counts level, wealth, health and dignity, skill, risk control and network. In the career's last quarterly phase take Lay low and reduce risk as the main action, and spend Energy first on Defuse legacy risk if the HR remark is not clear, then on Health recovery and Mental recovery until Health and Dignity are full, then on Network maintenance; never High-intensity work or Skill learning there. In the last months' stories pick the option that keeps you healthy, respected and out of trouble.
