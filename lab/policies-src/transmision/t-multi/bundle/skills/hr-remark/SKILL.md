# Answer the HR remark

Each month an HR partner comments on your risk. Only the smiling, all-right remark means clear. Any remark that mentions risk, worry or anger means hidden risk is piling up; it blows up when it gets high, and a second blow-up ends the run. While the remark is not the clear one: in the quarter take Lay low and reduce risk as the main action and Defuse legacy risk with Energy, until the clear remark comes back.

Why: the remark is the only visible reading of hidden risk; acting early is cheap, a blow-up costs Dignity, Wealth and half a year of Output.
