# No detonators under risk

While the HR remark is not the clear one, never pick an option that hides, denies, deceives, gossips, cuts corners, delays a disclosure, or puts you under formal scrutiny (formal complaints, investigations, open letters, escalating over your manager's head). These set off the buried risk at once. Prefer the option that discloses, fixes or closes the problem quietly.

Why: with risk already buried, an option that could expose it or invite scrutiny makes it go off on the spot; with a clear remark the same option is usually safe.
