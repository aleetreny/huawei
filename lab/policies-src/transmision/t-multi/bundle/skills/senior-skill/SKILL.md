# Build Skill at senior levels

From L4 on, Skill is the slow stat that holds promotion back; Network and Output keep up on their own. Every quarter at L4 or above, spend 2 Energy on Skill learning and take Apply for rotation as the main action, unless the risk rule or low Health says otherwise. In stories, prefer the option that teaches or builds capability.

Why: the handbook says higher levels set a higher bar; the bar that rises fastest is Skill, and quarterly Skill actions are the only steady source besides stories.
