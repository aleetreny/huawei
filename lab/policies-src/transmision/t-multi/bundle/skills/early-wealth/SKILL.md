# Do not go broke early

Pay is small in the first levels and Wealth starts near zero. While Wealth is only a few months of pay, refuse options that cost money (paying, buying, treating, lending, fees, gifts). Months with Wealth below zero end the run.

Why: early pay barely covers small losses, and an empty wallet held for a few months is a run-ending failure.
