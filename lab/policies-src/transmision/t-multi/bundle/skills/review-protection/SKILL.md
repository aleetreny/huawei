# Protect the review

Reviews come every six months. The review is settled as the review month begins, before that month's stories, so the story choices of the month just before a review month are the last ones that count. In that month never pick an option that may lower Skill, Network or Output, even if another option looks more principled or adds Dignity: Dignity and Health can be rebuilt later, a lost point can cost the promotion. Options that confront, correct or refuse someone in public usually cost Network; options that take time from your own delivery (extra unplanned work, investigations, rework, helping outside your role) usually cost Output.

Why: a promotion needs Skill, Network and Output all at the level's bar on the day of the review; in the first levels the bar sits at the top of what the level lets you hold, so there is no spare point.
