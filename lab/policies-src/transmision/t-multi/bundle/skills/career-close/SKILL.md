# Close the career well

The ending evaluation counts level, wealth, health and dignity, skill, risk control and network. In the last three months of the 48: bring the HR remark back to clear (Lay low and reduce risk, Defuse legacy risk), restore Health and Dignity with Energy, and in stories pick the option that keeps you healthy, respected and out of trouble; take on no new risk.

Why: in the last months there is no later review to play for; only the ending evaluation is left.
