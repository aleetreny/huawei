# Career rules

## How to use this Skill
- **Stories and quarterly phases are separate.** A story is decided only by "Story decisions" below. The quarterly list never picks a story option, and a story rule never picks an Energy or main action.
- Read this Skill again whenever its rules are no longer in view, for example after a summary or a resume.
- **What is published and what is ours.** The handbook publishes the levels, the six-monthly promotion reviews, the quarterly actions and their directions, pay and the failure directions; it does not publish formulas, thresholds or hidden-risk conditions. Lines below that say how options "usually" move a stat are our working assumptions for reading option text, not game rules. Trust what the game shows you (stat changes, the HR remark, review talks and warnings) over them.

## The 48 months at a glance
- **Every review (every six months).** Promotion is decided at the half-year review from Skill, Network and Output. The review talk arrives as a new six-month block starts (month 6, 12, 18 …), so the month before it (N = 1) is the last one whose choices count, and a single lost point there can cost the promotion. Prepare from two months out and protect the three stats in the last month (Story step 4).
- **A stalled start.** The handbook says two weak half-year reviews mean dismissal. If two reviews have passed at your starting level without a promotion, the next one is the last chance: until it, hidden risk comes first in every story and quarterly phase (Story step 2, Quarterly line 3) and Network and Output are kept at their Best.
- **Throughout.** Keep the HR remark friendly. **After a blow-up** (the game tells you the old landmines went off), play the rest of the run one notch safer: read every HR remark as one step worse than it says, in stories and quarterly phases, because the handbook says a second blow-up ends the run. The handbook warns that old landmines can go off at once and wipe out half a year of work, and that a second blow-up ends a run in a trust crisis. Keep Health and Dignity away from the bottom of their range, Wealth above zero and Network above zero: each is a published failure direction.
- **Growing.** Between review deadlines, grow the stats the next review needs. The handbook says higher levels set a higher bar and describes critical systems, leading teams and setting technical direction from level 4; our plan is to make Skill the quarterly priority at level 4 with the published actions that raise it (Skill learning, Apply for rotation; Quarterly line 5) and, from level 5, to buy Output as well with the cheap published actions (Prepare report materials, Apply for a key project; Quarterly line 6), because in our runs Output is the stat that lags at that level while Skill keeps growing in stories. Pay rises with level, so an earlier promotion is also the main source of Wealth.
- **End of the career.** The last review comes as month 48 starts, so month 47 is still a review month (N = 1). After it, the ending evaluation counts level, wealth, health and dignity, skill, risk control and network: in the last quarterly phase (month 48) lower risk and raise Health and Dignity instead of chasing growth.

## Turn loop
Every call resends the whole conversation, so the run stays within budget only if each decision costs one reply: tool calls in one reply run in order, and every result comes back before your next turn.
1. `observe` once. Read `events` first: the monthly HR remark, the review talk, warnings. If your last note is not in view (after a summary or a resume), call `check_latest_logs` with a small count, enough to find your most recent note, and copy its status line. This Skill already holds what you need from the handbook, so do not call `show_employee_handbook`.
2. Decide silently with the section for the current phase: a story offers options about a situation; a quarterly phase offers Energy actions (with their Energy cost) or main actions. Write no analysis: a reply contains tool calls only.
3. **Story: one reply with exactly two tool calls**, `take_action` (the exact `choice` and the one-line note) and then `observe`, which returns the next situation together with the result of the action. Never send `take_action` alone, and never send `observe` in a reply of its own. **Quarterly phase: one reply for the whole quarter.** The Energy choices and the main-action choices keep the same numbers every quarter, so after the one `observe` reply with `take_action` for each Energy action in turn (a typical quarter is one 2-Energy action plus one 1-Energy action, or three 1-Energy actions), then `take_action` for the main action, then `observe`.
   The shape of every story reply, with no text around it:
   ```
   tool_calls: [
     take_action(session_id="<id>", choice=<n>, notes="Review in N | Now S.. N.. O.. | Best S.. N.. O.. | Reviews here: 0 | <short reason>"),
     observe(session_id="<id>")
   ]
   ```
4. If a call returns `success` false, read the error, `observe` again and choose a valid option.

## Status line
The note is your memory: it survives summaries and comes back through `check_latest_logs`. Every note is one line: the status line, then at most one short reason.
`Review in N | Now S<Skill> N<Network> O<Output> | Best S<..> N<..> O<..> | Reviews here: 0`
- **Review in N:** N = 6 - (current_month mod 6), and N = 6 when current_month is a multiple of 6. Reviews come every six months (handbook); N = 1 is the last month before a review, the deadline for review preparation in our plan.
- **Now:** Skill, Network and Output as `observe` shows them before this choice. If you cannot find your last note, rebuild the line from `observe`: Best = Now, Reviews here = 0.
- **Best:** the highest value of each of the three you have seen at your current level (copy it from your last note and raise it when Now is higher). Reset it to Now when your level changes. A stat under its Best has lost ground since you last held it.
- **Reviews here:** how many half-year review talks you have received at your current level without a promotion, counting the one that arrived this month. Add 1 when a review talk arrives and your level has not changed; reset it to 0 when you are promoted. At your starting level, 2 means the stalled-start rule applies.

## Story decisions
Before choosing, read every option for the likely sign of its effect on Skill, Network, Output, Health, Dignity, Wealth and hidden risk. The game does not publish these effects; these are our working assumptions for reading option text, and the changes the game shows after a choice come first:
- **Skill** rises when you use your own judgement: analyse, bring data or evidence, prepare properly, think a decision through, learn, design a plan, negotiate with facts, coach someone with questions. This holds for options about people or pay too, not only code.
- **Output** rises when you deliver, ship, finish or cover the work, keep to your own deliverables or make your results visible. It falls when your time or credit goes elsewhere: learning, helping or organising for others, reviewing their work, giving the credit away, apologising and redoing, pausing a delivery, complaining instead of working. Output and Network often trade against each other.
- **Network** rises when you help, involve, credit or work with others; it falls when you confront, refuse, embarrass someone, go over their head or ignore them.
- **Dignity** rises when you act straight, stand your ground calmly, own a mistake or get credit; it falls when you are humiliated, cave in, get blamed or gossip.
- **Health** falls with overtime, weekends, extra load and stress; it rises with rest and boundaries. **Wealth** moves with pay, bonuses and money you spend.
- **Hidden risk** (not shown; the HR remark is your only gauge) is likely to rise with hiding, denying, blaming, shortcuts, gambles, fixing things privately or off the record, anonymous moves, side deals, partial, phased or postponed fixes, skipping necessary work to save time, open conflict, ignoring a known problem, disengaging, and challenging the company, its culture, leaders or rules even through a formal channel. It is likely to fall when you document, report a concrete problem openly, follow the process, own a mistake at once or fix the problem fully. Missing a risky option costs far more than flagging a safe one: when in doubt, treat it as risky.

Then apply these steps in order. Skip a step if it would remove every option.
1. **Survive.** Drop any option that could end the run now, for example by taking Health to zero.
2. **Risk.** Drop options that may add hidden risk. If the latest HR remark is anything but the friendly one, or you have already chosen an option this month that might bury a problem, also drop options that keep a problem private, anonymous, partial, phased or for later, or skip work to save time, even when they solve the immediate problem; prefer an option that documents, reports, owns or fully fixes it. During a stalled start (Reviews here is 2 at your starting level), prefer the options that lower risk even when another grows a stat faster.
   **If every option looks risky, the size of the risk decides, not the growth:** postponing, going round a problem or letting something go is a smaller risk than forcing, confronting or caving in, and far smaller than hiding, denying, blaming or gambling. Choose the smallest and most reversible one.
   **Exception for the last month before a review (N = 1) while the HR remark is friendly or quiet:** apply step 4 first. A mild risk (handling something privately, partially or later) is then acceptable when it is the only way to keep Skill, Network and Output, because a lost point there can cost the promotion. Hiding, denying, blaming, gambling and cutting corners stay out in every month, and the exception does not apply during a stalled start or after a blow-up.
3. **Wellbeing.** If Health or Dignity is low or falling, drop options that lower it. If Wealth is below zero, drop options that cost money. If Network is at zero, prefer an option that involves or helps someone.
4. **Review close (N = 1 or 2).** Promotions are lost here. With N = 2, drop options that may lower Network or Output. If Network or Output is under its Best, choose the honest option that most directly raises it back. With N = 1, apply all of this step. Drop options that may lower Skill, Network or Output: going against a person (blaming, correcting in public, refusing, reporting someone, going over a head) may cost Network; giving up time or credit from your own delivery (unplanned work, investigations, rework, the slow thorough path, someone else's problem) may cost Output; avoiding the work or the learning may cost Skill. Never save a point by hiding a problem or cutting a corner.
5. **Grow.** From the rest, choose the option that raises the most of Skill, Network and Output, counting twice a stat the latest review talk named as lagging, and minus one for each of them it lowers. In the final quarter of the career, prefer Health, Dignity and lower risk instead (step 4 still comes first in month 47, the last review month). On a tie: lower risk, then more Health and Dignity, then the option that costs no money.

## Quarterly phases only: first matching line wins
These lines choose Energy and main actions only, never a story option. You have 3 Energy per quarter; Skill learning, High-intensity work and Defuse legacy risk cost 2, the others 1. Unused Energy is lost. Plan the whole quarter after one `observe` and send all its `take_action` calls in one reply (Turn loop, step 3).
1. **Last quarter of the career (month 48):** plan Energy and the main action together. Energy: Defuse legacy risk once if the HR remark is not the friendly one, then, with the Energy left, Health recovery and Mental recovery while either is below full, then Network maintenance. Main action: Lay low and reduce risk if the HR remark is not the friendly one, otherwise Report results to your manager. No Skill learning or High-intensity work.
2. **Near a limit.** Health or Dignity in the lower part of its 0–10 range (our margin: 4 or below, not a published limit) or falling three months in a row; Wealth below zero; or Network at zero. These are published failure directions, and the game warns before they end a run. Recover first: Energy on Health recovery, Mental recovery or Network maintenance, main action Repair a key relationship (or Lay low and reduce risk for Health), and avoid spending. No Skill learning or High-intensity work until it recovers. Health at zero ends the run. One exception: if the HR remark is not the friendly one, Defuse legacy risk still takes the first 2 Energy (line 3) and the recovery action takes the third, because the handbook's blow-up wipes out half a year, more than a low stat recovers in a quarter.
3. **HR remark** (classify it by its tone, whatever the exact wording). Friendly ("you are doing all right"): no risk action. Quiet ("some risk, nothing serious yet"): first Energy action Defuse legacy risk, main action from the lines below. Anything else (frowning, uneasy, "you are on your own", angry): first Energy action Defuse legacy risk and main action Lay low and reduce risk, every quarter until the remark is friendly or quiet again. After a blow-up, read the remark one step worse for the rest of the run: friendly counts as quiet, quiet as frowning. During a stalled start, treat the quiet remark like the others (Defuse legacy risk and Lay low and reduce risk) and spend the Energy left on whichever of Output or Network is under its Best.
4. **A review talk named a lagging stat:** until the next review, spend Energy and the main action on it (Prepare report materials for Output, Network maintenance for Network, Skill learning for Skill; main action Apply for a key project, or Apply for rotation when it is Skill).
5. **Level 4:** Skill first, because the handbook says the bar rises with level and describes critical systems, leading teams and setting technical direction from here, and Skill learning and Apply for rotation are the published actions that raise Skill. Energy: Skill learning (2) while Health is above 4 (the margin of line 2), then one point (1) on Network maintenance, or on Prepare report materials when Network is at its Best and Output is under it; if Skill learning was skipped for Health, Health recovery (1) and the two 1-cost actions. Main action Apply for rotation. Lines 1 to 4 still come first.
6. **Level 5 or above:** Skill and Output together, because the next bar needs both and in our runs Output is the one stories barely move at this level. Energy: Skill learning (2) while Health is above 4, then Prepare report materials (1); if Skill learning was skipped for Health, Prepare report materials and Health recovery. Main action Apply for a key project (Output, Skill and Network). Lines 1 to 4 still come first.
7. **Otherwise (levels 1 to 3):** spend Energy on Skill, Network and Output (Prepare report materials, Network maintenance, Skill learning while Health is above 4), starting with whichever of Network or Output is under its Best. Main action Apply for a key project.
