# Climb: feed the bottleneck

Each promotion raises the bar of the next review. Stories raise Skill, Network and Output unevenly, so one of them usually blocks the next promotion while the others keep rising for nothing. Quarter-end actions are the part you control: aim them at the blocker.

## Find the blocker, in this order
1. **The review told you.** If the most recent review talk, your manager or a warning named a weakness (results or delivery: Output; relationships or collaboration: Network; capability, skills or keeping up: Skill), that input is the blocker until the next review.
2. **A review passed without promotion while Network and Output were still rising:** the blocker is Skill. At higher levels Skill is the slowest input to build, so treat it as the blocker from then on unless a review names something else.
3. **Otherwise:** the input that has risen least since your last promotion, ignoring any that has stalled.

## At every quarter-end after your first promotion
After any Health or risk need (Health recovery when Health is in the lower half, Mental recovery when Dignity is; Defuse legacy risk after a risk remark):
- **Skill blocks:** Skill learning while Health is above the middle of its range, and Apply for rotation or Build a mentorship as the main action. Every quarter counts: Skill takes many of them.
- **Output blocks:** Prepare report materials with every remaining point; Apply for a key project or Report results to your manager.
- **Network blocks:** Network maintenance with every remaining point; Build a mentorship or Report results to your manager.
Never spend Energy or a main action on an input that has stalled. Write the blocker in your note: `blocker: Skill`.

## In stories
When two honest answers differ mainly in what they build, prefer the one that builds the blocker.
