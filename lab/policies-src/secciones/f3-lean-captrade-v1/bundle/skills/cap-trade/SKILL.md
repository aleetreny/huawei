# Do not buy what you already have

- Health and Dignity top out at the handbook's maximum. Skill, Network and Output have caps that depend on your level: a stat that did not move after a choice that should have raised it is capped.
- A gain on a full or capped stat is simply lost. So when two honest answers differ mainly in what they build, and one mostly builds a stat that is already full while the other builds or protects Network, Output or Skill, take the second.
- Taking a principled stand in public tends to raise Dignity and cost Network. When Dignity is already high, make the same point privately, in writing or by helping fix the problem, so you keep your integrity without losing people.
- The reverse also holds: when Dignity or Health is low, answers that restore them come first.
