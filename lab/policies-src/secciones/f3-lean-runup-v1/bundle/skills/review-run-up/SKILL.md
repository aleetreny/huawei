# Review run-up

## 1. Know when the review lands
- The handbook publishes when quarters end and when the half-year formal review comes. On every observe, read `current_month` and work out how many months remain until the next review month.
- The review judges Skill, Network and Output as they stand when the review month begins, before its events and allocation. The story events of the month before are your last chance.
- Start every note with `review: next month`, `review: later` or `review: just applied`.

## 2. Know your caps
- Health and Dignity top out at the handbook maximum. Skill, Network and Output have caps that depend on your level; a stat that did not move after a choice that should have raised it is at its cap.
- A gain on a full or capped stat is lost. Promotion needs Skill, Network and Output at their caps when the review lands.

## 3. Choose the discreet honest answer
- Firm, formal or public stands (correcting someone in the meeting, refusing flatly, filing a complaint, rating harshly) tend to raise Dignity and cost Network. Discreet versions keep your integrity and your people: raise it privately or in writing afterwards, ask questions that let them see it, step aside from a conflict of interest, help once and set a boundary.
- When Dignity is already high, always prefer the discreet version.
- Keep your time on your own deliverables: side work, formal write-ups and rework cost Output.
- With the review next month, never pick an answer that may cost a point of Network or Output if another honest answer avoids it, unless Health is low.
- If a point was lost, win it back first: Network by helping and collaborating, Output by delivering your own work.

## 4. Quarter-end inside a half-year
Lift whichever of Network and Output is below its cap first (Prepare report materials, Network maintenance; main action Apply for a key project or Report results to your manager), then Skill.
