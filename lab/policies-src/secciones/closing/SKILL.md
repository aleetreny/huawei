# Close the career well

The run lasts the handbook's fixed number of months, and the ending summary looks at where you stand when it ends: your level and wealth, but also Health and Dignity, Skill, Network, and how much risk is still buried.

## In the final year
- If a promotion is still within reach at one of the remaining reviews, keep building for it first; the closing steps below come on top.
- Risk: what is buried at the end is what your answers created, so in stories take no answer that hides, delays or shortcuts anything, and keep using Defuse legacy risk after any risk remark until the very end.
- Health and Dignity: keep both in the upper half. Use Health recovery or Mental recovery with spare Energy, and avoid answers that cost them.
- Network and Skill: do not trade them away for comfort; prefer answers that help colleagues and build capability.
- Wealth: avoid answers that cost money.

## Health to the very end
Unless Health is already near the top, keep a Health recovery point in every remaining allocation: an exhausted career ends at once, even in its last months.
