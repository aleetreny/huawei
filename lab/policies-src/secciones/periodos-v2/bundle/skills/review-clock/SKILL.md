# Review clock

## Where you are in the cycle
- The handbook publishes the timeline: when quarters end and when the half-year formal review comes. On every observe, read `current_month` and work out from that timeline how many months remain until the next review month.
- The review judges the Skill, Network and Output you have built before it. Treat the story choices of the month just before a review month as your last chance, and do not count on the review month's own events or allocation.
- Start every note with the countdown: `review: next month`, `review: later` or `review: just applied`.

## Stalled stats
At each level a stat can stall: a choice that should have raised it leaves it unchanged. Network and Output stall early, Skill later. A stalled stat is as high as this level allows, so every point lost there must be won back before the review, and there may be no time.

## Story choices
- Stay honest, but keep people with you: raise a concern privately or in writing, help the person fix it, give credit. Confronting someone in public, refusing flatly or filing a formal complaint usually costs Network.
- Keep your own deliverables first: answers that pull you away from your own work cost Output.
- With a review one month away, never pick an answer that may cost a point of a stalled Network or Output if another honest answer avoids it, even when that other answer costs some Dignity or comfort, or a little Health while Health is still in the upper half. Those recover; the lost point costs the promotion. Health near the bottom always comes first: an exhausted career ends at once.
- If a point was lost earlier in the half-year, win it back first: Network by helping and collaborating, Output by delivering your own work.

## Quarter-end before your first promotion
After any recovery or risk need, lift whichever of Network and Output has not stalled first (Prepare report materials, Network maintenance; main action Apply for a key project or Report results to your manager), then Skill.
