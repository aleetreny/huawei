# Close the career well

The handbook fixes the length of the run; the career summary reflects where you stand when it ends, not only how high you climbed.

## Once the end is in sight
1. **Still promotable?** If a remaining review can still promote you, keep following review-clock or climb first; the steps below come on top.
2. **Risk.** What is buried at the end is what your answers created. Take no answer that hides, delays or shortcuts anything, and keep using Defuse legacy risk after any risk remark until the very last allocation.
3. **Health and Dignity.** Keep both in the upper half: Health recovery or Mental recovery with spare Energy, and no answers that cost them. An exhausted career ends at once, however close the end is.
4. **Network and Skill.** Do not trade them away for comfort; prefer answers that help colleagues and build capability.
5. **Wealth.** Avoid answers that cost money.
