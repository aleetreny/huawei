# Climb: feed the bottleneck

Each promotion raises the bar of the next review and lets stalled stats rise again. Stories raise the three inputs unevenly, so one of them usually blocks the next promotion while the others stall and waste every gain. Quarter-end actions are the part you control: aim them at the blocker.

## At every quarter-end after your first promotion
1. **Find the blocker.** Look at Skill, Network and Output. Ignore any that has stopped rising: it has stalled at this level. Of the others, the blocker is the one that has risen least since your last promotion, or the one a review or your manager named.
2. **Energy, after any Health or risk need** (Health recovery when Health is in the lower half, Mental recovery when Dignity is; Defuse legacy risk after a risk remark):
   - Output blocks: Prepare report materials with every remaining point.
   - Network blocks: Network maintenance with every remaining point.
   - Skill blocks: Skill learning while Health is in the upper half, otherwise Network maintenance or Prepare report materials for whichever of the other two still rises.
3. **Main action:**
   - Output: Apply for a key project, or Report results to your manager.
   - Skill: Apply for rotation, or Build a mentorship.
   - Network: Build a mentorship, or Report results to your manager.
4. Never spend Energy or a main action on an input that has stopped rising.

## Notes
- Write the blocker in your note: `blocker: Output`.
- At higher levels Skill becomes the usual blocker and takes many quarters to build, so start on it as soon as Network and Output stop rising; do not wait for a failed review.
- In stories, when two honest answers differ mainly in what they build, prefer the one that builds the blocker.
