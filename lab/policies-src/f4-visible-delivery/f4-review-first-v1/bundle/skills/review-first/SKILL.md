# Review first

Weak performance reviews are the main threat to this career: repeated weak reviews can end the run. One priority governs every decision: a strong next review, earned with visible Output and results the manager knows about. Leave it only for an exception below, and return as soon as it clears.

## Turn loop
`observe` → check the exceptions → decide → `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note that starts with the mode ("deliver", "review emergency" or "exception: Health"). If `success` is false, observe and pick an affordable option. Never start a second game. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Exceptions
- Health or Dignity is clearly in danger: in the lower part of its 0–10 range, or named in a warning. Repair it first: Health recovery or Lay low and reduce risk for Health, Mental recovery for Dignity.
- Network is at or near zero: Network maintenance.
- Feedback warns of risk, complaints or an unresolved past problem: Defuse legacy risk in the next Energy phase.

A slow decline alone is not an exception; keep delivering.

## Quarterly Energy
Read `energy_cost`, `status_updates` and `remaining_energy`; never pick an option that costs more than the Energy left. Cover any exception first. Spend the rest on what adds the most Output: Prepare report materials, plus High-intensity work while Health and Dignity are clearly out of danger.

## Quarterly main action
Default: Report results to your manager. Take Apply for a key project instead when feedback points to capability or Skill has stopped rising. Under a Health exception: Lay low and reduce risk.

## Story events
Prefer the option that completes work, takes ownership and makes results visible to the manager. Never cut corners, hide a problem or claim credit you did not earn: such shortcuts build hidden risk that can later end the run. When every option has a cost, let it fall on a dimension that is not in danger.

## Reviews
Each review judges the whole half-year, so deliver in every quarter instead of saving effort. After a C or D, or feedback that results fell short, the next review is an emergency: every spare Energy point and main action goes to Output and reporting, aimed at the gap the feedback names. After a promotion expectations rise; keep the same default.
