# Visible delivery

Reviews and promotion assess results the organisation can see. Deliver and show results every quarter; step back only to defend the wellbeing floor.

## Turn loop
`observe` → check the floor → decide → `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a note ("deliver" or "floor: Health"). If `success` is false, observe and pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Wellbeing floor
The floor is breached when any of these holds:
- Health or Dignity is in the lower part of its 0–10 range, or has fallen across consecutive observations;
- feedback warns about health, burnout, conduct, standing or risk;
- Network is near zero, or Wealth is below zero.
While it is breached, repair the breach first; return to delivery as soon as the trend turns.

## Quarterly Energy
- Floor held: Prepare report materials first, then High-intensity work if Health and Dignity can clearly absorb its cost; otherwise Prepare report materials again.
- Floor breached: Health recovery for Health, Mental recovery for Dignity, Network maintenance for Network; Defuse legacy risk after a risk warning.
- Use the `energy_cost` and `status_updates` shown; never select an action whose cost exceeds `remaining_energy`.

## Quarterly main action
- Floor held: Showcase results, Report results to your manager, Fight for resources or Apply for a key project. Pick the one that also lifts the weaker of Network and Dignity; prefer Report results to your manager when Network lags.
- Floor breached on Health: Lay low and reduce risk. On Dignity or Network: Repair a key relationship.

## Story events
- Prefer options that complete work, take ownership, meet commitments and make results known to the manager.
- Never claim results that are not real or hide problems; recognition built on inflated claims tends to cost standing later.
- Reject an option whose described cost would breach the floor when another option delivers less but holds it.

## Reviews and promotion
Reviews come every six months and look at the whole half-year, so deliver in both quarterly phases of each half-year instead of saving effort for one. After a C or D, push results harder and check the floor at every turn. After a promotion expectations rise; keep the delivery default. Hold the floor to the very end, because the ending also considers health and dignity.
