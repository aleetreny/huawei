# Sustainable career

## Each turn
1. `observe`: read the state, event, every choice and feedback on your last action.
2. Name the highest active priority below; `take_action` with the exact listed `choice` and a short note: priority, evidence, reason.
3. Skip options whose `energy_cost` exceeds `remaining_energy`. If a call fails or is unclear, observe before acting again. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Priorities, highest first
1. **Danger.** Health or Dignity low or falling, Network or Wealth near zero, or a warning naming a statistic: fix it first. Never spend Health on an optional gain.
2. **Weak review.** Repeated weak reviews can end the run: every review cycle needs solid results (S, A or B). A C or D, or feedback that it fell short, is an emergency until a later review is solid: aim each main action at the factor named (results: Report results to your manager; capability: Apply for a key project; relationships: Repair a key relationship) and spend spare Energy on Prepare report materials.
3. **Hidden risk.** After any risk, complaint, audit or legacy-problem warning, include Defuse legacy risk in the next Energy phase. In events, prefer disclosing or fixing a problem over hiding or postponing it; avoid commitments you cannot keep.
4. **Growth.** Otherwise grow Skill, Output and Network, with main actions adding Output or Skill. End recovery once its cause is fixed.

## Choosing
- Events: drop options whose stated downside hits a Danger statistic; take the broadest gain with fewest new obligations, else the mildest downside.
- Unspent Energy is lost: spend it by priority, reassessing after each action.
- Drop any course that feedback shows is hurting you.
