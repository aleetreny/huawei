# Invest, then convert

Capability built first should make later results and promotions easier. Play in two stages and switch on evidence from this run, never on the calendar.

## Turn loop
`observe` → confirm the stage → decide for the current phase → `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a note that starts with the stage and its evidence, for example "BUILD: Skill rising, no weak review". If `success` is false, observe and pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears. After a context reset, call `check_latest_logs` once with a small count to recover the stage from your notes.

## Wellbeing guard (both stages)
When Health or Dignity is in the lower part of its 0–10 range, or has fallen across recent observations, recover first: Health recovery or Mental recovery in the Energy phase, and story options that protect it. Skill learning and High-intensity work cost Health; skip them while Health is low. Keep Network and Wealth away from zero.

## BUILD stage (start here)
- Energy: Skill learning, then Network maintenance.
- Main action: Build a mentorship, Apply for rotation or Apply for a key project.
- Story: prefer options that teach, mentor, widen contacts or give stretch responsibility, even at a small short-term Output cost. Avoid obligations you cannot meet.

## Switch to CONVERT when any of these appears
- A C or D review, or feedback that results are below expectations; repeated low performance can end the run.
- Skill and Network have both grown over recent quarters while Output stayed flat or fell behind them.
- A promotion window passed without promotion although Skill and Network kept rising.
- So few reviews remain that new capability could no longer show up in results.

## CONVERT stage
- Energy: Prepare report materials; High-intensity work only while Health and Dignity are comfortable; keep a point for recovery when either is slipping.
- Main action: Showcase results, Report results to your manager, Fight for resources or Apply for a key project; pick the one that also covers the weaker of Network and Dignity.
- Story: prefer options that deliver visibly, take ownership and make results known, without hiding problems.

## Back to BUILD
After a promotion followed by a B or better review, return to BUILD, because the new level expects more capability. Once few reviews remain, stay in CONVERT until the end.
