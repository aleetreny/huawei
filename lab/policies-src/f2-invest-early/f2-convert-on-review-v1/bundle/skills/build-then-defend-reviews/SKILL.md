# Build early, defend every review

Early capability pays later only if no further weak review follows. Switch on this run's feedback, never on the calendar.

## Turn loop
`observe` → update Stage and Risk → decide → `take_action` with the `choice` number and a one-line note starting with both, e.g. "CONVERT, Raised: C review". If `success` is false, observe and pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears. After a context reset, call `check_latest_logs` once with a small count to recover Stage and Risk.

## Order when Energy is short
1. Wellbeing: if Health or Dignity is in the lower part of its 0–10 range or falling, use Health recovery or Mental recovery, skipping Skill learning and High-intensity work. Keep Network and Wealth above zero.
2. Risk.
3. Stage.

## Stage
**BUILD** (start here)
- Energy: Skill learning, then Network maintenance.
- Main action: Build a mentorship, Apply for rotation or Apply for a key project.
- Story: prefer options that teach, mentor, widen contacts or stretch responsibility; avoid obligations you cannot meet.

**Switch to CONVERT at once** on a C or D review, or feedback that results fall short or the next review must improve. Repeated weak reviews can end the run, so treat the first as an emergency. Also switch when Output lags clearly behind grown Skill and Network, a promotion window passes without promotion, or the run nears its end.

**CONVERT**
- Energy: Prepare report materials with every free point.
- Main action: Report results to your manager or Showcase results.
- Story: prefer options that deliver visibly, meet commitments and make results known.
- If the next grade is still weak, stay and aim the main action at what the feedback names: Build a mentorship for capability, Repair a key relationship for relationships.

**Leave CONVERT** only after a B or better review, never near the end. Back in BUILD, Prepare report materials replaces Network maintenance.

## Risk (starts Clear)
- **Raised**: a warning; HR, compliance, complaint, audit or legacy-problem wording; or a rising risk entry in `status_updates`. Next Energy phase: Defuse legacy risk first. In stories prefer options that disclose, fix or close a problem over postponing, hiding or shifting it.
- **Escalating**: repeated or stronger signals. Defuse legacy risk every Energy phase; take no new commitments; outside CONVERT, main action Lay low and reduce risk or Repair a key relationship.
- Step down one level after a quarter without a new signal.
