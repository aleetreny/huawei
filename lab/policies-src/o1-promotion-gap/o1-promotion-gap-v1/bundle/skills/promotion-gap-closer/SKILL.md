# Promotion gap closer

Belief: promotion is a gate, and the stat lagging furthest behind your level keeps it shut. The handbook says the bar depends on Skill, Network, Output and level. Level is also the wealth engine: base pay and every review bonus scale with it (published tables), so each missed promotion window delays every later raise.

## Keep a gap diagnosis
Name one binding gap: Skill, Network or Output. Revise it only on new evidence: grades, manager feedback, a missed promotion. Jargon you cannot follow points to Skill; work stalling for lack of help points to Network; being overlooked points to Output. If time at your level keeps growing without promotion, switch to the next weakest stat.

## One plan per promotion window
- Energy round right after a review: build the gap. Skill: Skill learning, plus Apply for rotation or Build a mentorship. Network: Network maintenance, plus Build a mentorship or Repair a key relationship. Output: High-intensity work, plus Apply for a key project.
- Energy round just before the next review: harvest. Prepare report materials, then Report results to your manager or Showcase results, so recent work is visible when the bar is checked.
- No clear gap: Apply for a key project, which raises all three.
- Spend all Energy; it does not carry over.

## Guardrails
Recovery is a tool, not a habit: Health recovery, Mental recovery or Lay low only when Health or Dignity is low or keeps falling, or after a warning. Two weak reviews end a career, so after one weak grade put the next round into Output and visibility. When events hint that old problems are piling up, spend Energy on Defuse legacy risk before they blow up.

## Story choices
Pick the option that grows the gap stat or visible Output without leaving hidden problems: own and fix issues openly, no shortcuts. Accept money that costs nothing toward promotion; never let Wealth run empty.

## Notes and efficiency
With each action write one short note: the current gap and how this choice closes it. Observe once per decision; check logs only after reviews, warnings or surprises.
