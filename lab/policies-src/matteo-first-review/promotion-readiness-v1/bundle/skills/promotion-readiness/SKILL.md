---
name: promotion-readiness
description: Prepare balanced promotion readiness with realised gains, dignity, capacity and fulfilled commitments.
---

Prepare for the first promotion by developing capability, usable working relationships and credited delivery together. Identify the weakest of these from the current state, actual changes and feedback. Prefer feasible work that produces a verifiable result and makes ownership clear. Further technical learning is a poor substitute for missing delivery or collaboration.

Work backward from the actual review boundary. Read the handbook, but verify when automatic reviews appear as the clock advances. Commit the necessary work and preparation before the boundary; the next quarter's allocation may occur after the decision. Check the promotion notice directly rather than treating a performance grade as the promotion outcome.

Maintain both physical capacity and professional self-respect. Preserve enough capacity to handle further events before the next recovery opportunity. Do not use repeated overtime or accepting other people's work as the default route to recognition. Low dignity is a concern even when capability, relationships and output appear strong.

Treat every promise, disclosure, shortcut and interpersonal decision as a possible future obligation. Keep a short ledger: what was promised, who owns the next step, when to follow up, and what remains unresolved. Protect confidential information, correct misleading claims and report material delivery risks with a feasible plan. Do not exchange a temporary relationship gain for concealment or an obligation that cannot be met.

Read changing warnings and inspect realised effects after acting. If accumulated exposure is worsening, resolve its source and use the available risk-reduction actions while preserving essential delivery. If an investment repeatedly stops improving the relevant statistic, stop repeating it automatically: put the scarce resource into a remaining gap, recovery or risk resolution. A displayed positive action effect is not a guarantee of realised growth.

At the review, inspect grade, promotion, warnings and survival separately. Help from a colleague is useful evidence of support, not proof that a particular sponsor or story is required. After promotion, continue protecting capacity and resolving obligations: a promotion notice does not prevent a simultaneous collapse.
