# Steady balance by status line

Keep every dimension out of persistent weakness; repair a slide before it compounds.

## Each turn
1. `observe`. Read the statistics and the `events` feedback; compare with earlier observations.
2. Fill the status line `Act: <dimensions or none> | Risk: calm or raised | Weakest: <statistic>`.
   - **Act**: Health or Dignity in the lower part of its 0–10 range; Network or Wealth near zero; a dimension named in a warning.
   - **Risk raised**: since the last Energy phase, buried problems blew up, or the latest feedback about risk is anything but reassuring.
   - **Weakest**: a falling statistic, else the lowest of Skill, Network and Output.
3. Need: Act if any, then raised Risk, then Weakest.
4. `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and the note "<status line> → need → why this option".
5. If `success` is false, observe and pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Serving each need
- **Act**. Energy: Health recovery, Mental recovery or Network maintenance for that dimension. Story: rule out options whose stated downside hits it, unless all do; then the mildest.
- **Risk raised**. Energy: Defuse legacy risk, however mild the wording.
- **Weakest**. Energy: the action that lifts it without lowering an Act dimension; Skill learning and High-intensity work cost Health, so only while Health is not Act. Story: the option that helps it, then the broadest modest gain; a steady gain beats a large gain with a lasting cost. Main action: cover the Weakest that Energy did not reach.
- Always: prefer honest, workable commitments over postponing a problem or adding risk you cannot see. Lay low and reduce risk only when Health is Act. Serve the needs in order until Energy is spent; unused Energy is lost.
