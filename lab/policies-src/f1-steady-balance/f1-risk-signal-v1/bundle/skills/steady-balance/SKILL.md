# Steady balance

Keep every dimension out of persistent weakness; repair a slide before it compounds.

## Each turn
1. `observe`. Read the statistics and the `events` feedback; compare with earlier observations to see each direction.
2. Classify:
   - **Act**: Health or Dignity in the lower part of its 0–10 range; Network or Wealth near zero; a dimension named in a warning.
   - **Weakest**: a falling statistic, else the lowest of Skill, Network and Output.
   - **Risk signal**: since the last Energy phase, buried problems blew up, or the latest feedback about risk is anything but reassuring.
3. `take_action` with the exact `choice` number and a short note, e.g. "act Health; risk signal; weakest Output".
4. If `success` is false, observe and pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Story events
- Rule out options whose stated downside hits an Act dimension, unless all do; then take the mildest.
- Otherwise prefer the option that helps the weakest, then the broadest modest gain. A steady gain beats a large gain with a lasting cost.
- Prefer honest, workable commitments over postponing a problem or adding risk you cannot see.

## Quarterly Energy
1. Act first: Health recovery for Health, Mental recovery for Dignity, Network maintenance for Network.
2. Risk signal next: Defuse legacy risk, however mild the wording.
3. Then lift the weakest without lowering an Act dimension. Skill learning and High-intensity work cost Health; use them only while Health is not Act.
4. Unused Energy is lost: spend it while a useful affordable action remains.

## Quarterly main action
Cover the weakest dimension Energy did not reach. Choose Lay low and reduce risk only when Health is Act.
