# Steady balance

Keep every dimension out of persistent weakness, repair slides early and grow steadily.

## Each turn
1. `observe`. Note each statistic, its trend, and the `events` feedback.
2. Update the mode and classify dimensions:
   - **Act**: Health or Dignity in the lower part of its 0–10 range; Network or Wealth near zero; named in a warning.
   - **Watch**: falling, or the lowest of Skill, Network and Output.
   - **Fine**: the rest.
3. `take_action` with the exact `choice` number; start the short note with the mode and its evidence, e.g. "RISK: blow-up; act Health".
4. If `success` is false, observe and pick an affordable option. Stop when `ending_score` appears.

## Modes
REVIEW and RISK can overlap and override the rules below; otherwise the mode is BALANCE.
- **REVIEW** starts with a C or D grade or feedback that performance is below expectations; repeated weak reviews can end the run. Main action: Report results to your manager, Showcase results or Apply for a key project; if the next grade is still weak, switch to another. Spare Energy: Prepare report materials, plus High-intensity work while Health and Dignity are Fine. Ends at a B or better.
- **RISK** starts with any warning about accumulated, legacy or hidden risk, trust or compliance, or a blow-up; accumulated risk can end the run. Defuse legacy risk in every Energy phase unless Act recovery leaves too little Energy. In story events rule out options that cut corners, hide, delay or shift a problem; prefer disclosing, fixing or closing it. Ends after a whole quarter without a new risk warning.
- When an emergency ends, note it and resume the normal rules.

## Story events
- Rule out options whose stated downside hits an Act dimension, unless all do; then take the mildest.
- Otherwise prefer the option helping the weakest dimension, then the broadest modest gain; a steady gain beats a large gain with a lasting cost.
- Prefer honest, workable commitments over postponing problems or adding unseen risk.

## Quarterly Energy
1. Act first: Health recovery, Mental recovery for Dignity, Network maintenance.
2. Then lift the weakest Watch dimension without lowering another; Skill learning and High-intensity work cost Health, so use them only while Health is Fine.
3. Spread points; unused Energy is lost.

## Quarterly main action
Cover the weakest dimension Energy did not reach. Lay low and reduce risk only when Health is Act.

## Across the run
Balance also drives promotion. Late in the run take no new risks: a failure costs more than a last gain.
