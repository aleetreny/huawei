# Sponsors and visibility

Belief: people decide promotions and review grades. Work your manager and senior colleagues know about and want to credit counts for more than work nobody sees. Build backers, keep them informed, stay useful to them. Higher level raises pay and bonus (published tables), so sponsorship is also the wealth plan.

## Read every event by roles
Name who is involved: manager, senior leader, peer, other team, customer, junior. Ask who owns the problem, who will remember your choice, and whether it makes your manager look good. Prefer options that:
- solve a problem for someone senior or another team, openly;
- share credit while making your part visible;
- raise bad news early with your manager instead of hiding it; a hidden problem that blows up costs trust, and a second blow-up ends a career;
- skip fights that cost a relationship for a small gain.
Accept money that costs no relationship; keep Wealth above empty.

## Energy: wear-free visibility first
Default: Prepare report materials and Network maintenance, which grow promotion stats and Dignity without costing Health, plus one more point where you lag. When Skill or Output clearly trails what your level needs, swap in Skill learning or High-intensity work. Recover only when Health or Dignity is low. When events hint that old problems are piling up, use Defuse legacy risk.

## Main actions
Rotate Apply for a key project (backers notice owners of key work) with Report results to your manager. Build a mentorship when Skill lags; Repair a key relationship when someone important has cooled. In the quarter just before each review, Report results to your manager or Showcase results. After a weak review, visibility and Output come first: two weak reviews end a career.

## Notes and efficiency
With each action write one short note: who it affects and what it signals. Observe once per decision; check logs only after reviews, warnings or surprises.
