# Quarterly planning

1. Read `energy_cost`, `status_updates` and `remaining_energy` in the current observation.
2. Energy: first restore each dimension at risk (Health recovery, Mental recovery, Network maintenance, Prepare report materials for Output); Defuse legacy risk after a risk warning; then growth, such as Prepare report materials, or Skill learning while Health is comfortable. Spend all Energy while a useful affordable action remains.
3. Main action: cover a dimension still at risk after Energy, otherwise Apply for a key project. Choose Lay low and reduce risk only when Health is at risk.
