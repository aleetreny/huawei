# Story decisions

1. Rule out options whose described downside worsens a dimension at risk, unless all do; then take the mildest downside. During promotion drive, apply this only to Health, Dignity and Wealth.
2. During promotion drive, take the option that best shows delivered results or raises your standing with decision makers at a bounded cost, over options that only keep things comfortable. Never hide a problem; after strong or repeated risk warnings, close problems first.
3. Otherwise prefer honest, workable commitments that help the weakest dimension; between similar options, the one that builds Skill, Network or visible Output without a lasting cost.
