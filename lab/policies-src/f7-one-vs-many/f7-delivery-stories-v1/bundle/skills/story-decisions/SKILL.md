# Story decisions

1. Rule out options whose described downside worsens a dimension at risk, unless all do; then take the mildest downside.
2. While Output or Network is below its recent high, prefer finishing, fixing or showing your own work, or settling things directly with the people involved, over side duties, other people's processes or delays, unless delivering would hide a problem or Health is low.
3. Prefer options that keep commitments honest and workable and that help the weakest dimension.
4. Between similar options, prefer the one that builds Skill, Network or visible Output without a lasting cost.
