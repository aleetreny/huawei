# Playbook card decisions — 26 October 2026

Source review of the 36 cards in `research/competition-playbook/` by six reviewers (six cards each), with one adversarial challenger per group who re-checked the primary sources and the cost case. Full outputs, including checked URLs: [playbook-review-2026-10-26.json](playbook-review-2026-10-26.json). The reviews were written before the user decision of 26 October that opened Claude workers on the real dev game. Cards affected by that decision were re-decided by the orchestrator; the reason is shown in the note.

Origin and execution stay separate. A card is an idea origin (`idea_origin: playbook:<card>`). An experiment exists only once it has a registry job or decision. Cards marked hypothesis keep their trigger; nothing here claims a champion's mechanism improves this game.

Overall source quality: most cards are **partly** supported. The cases are real, but most causal readings ("they won because of X") are the authors' or our interpretation. The strongest transfers are operational (P29, P33, P36, P01 interface check), not strategic.
