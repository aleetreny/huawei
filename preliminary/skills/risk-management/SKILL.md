# Risk Management

## Purpose
Prevent avoidable early termination and persistent deterioration that would undermine the 48-month career outcome. This is a Skill for the same single decision-making agent.

## Risk audit
1. Read the current officially observed statistics, warnings, and recent relevant logs. Distinguish a momentary dip from sustained deterioration, while taking acute dangers seriously.
2. Consider the published failure directions: Health reaching zero; prolonged critically low Dignity; serious accumulated organisational risk; repeated weak performance; prolonged zero Network; prolonged negative Wealth; and Skill lagging organisational expectations. Exact internal triggers are deliberately unknown.
3. Identify whether the proposed choice would worsen an already fragile dimension, especially if the adverse effect could persist over several months.
4. If the proposed choice compounds a major risk, compare lower-risk available alternatives, including recovery, relationship repair, output stabilisation, and risk reduction in quarterly phases.
5. Where no option is clearly safe, prefer the option with the more manageable and reversible downside, based on the information actually given. Do not fabricate certainty about ambiguous story outcomes.
6. Following a warning, performance review, or significant negative result, reassess the next actions. Return to a growth-oriented approach when the observed situation is sufficiently stable.

## Decision principles
- Survival has priority over chasing a single promotion or an isolated short-term benefit.
- Health, Dignity, Network, Wealth, performance, Skill and output matter together. No one metric should be maximised at the expense of persistent collapse in another.
- Use qualitative risk signals and changes over time. Do not state undocumented numeric thresholds, hidden counters, or event trigger probabilities.
- Only select among official available choices. Use official MCP tools and their returned data, never internal data files or engine functions.
- Make the final recommendation brief so the career-planning Skill can combine it with development priorities efficiently.
